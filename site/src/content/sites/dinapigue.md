## What the images show

In the 2003–2006 composite, forest runs unbroken from the ridges down to the Pacific shore. By 2026 a
haul road runs north–south through the forest, linking several bare, orange-brown clearings where
laterite ore is dug, with more clearing near the coast in the southeast. The magenta layer marks every
pixel that went from green vegetation to bare ground; the sea is left out.

The Sentinel-2 pair starts in 2016–2018, when the roads and several clearings already existed. Most of the
change since then is a new clearing in the middle of the box and widened roads.


## Who lives here

Researchers counted about 370 Agta residents in Dinapigue in 2015, mainly in Ayod, Digumased and Dibulo.
Families combined fishing, hunting, gathering and farming with paid work, including work for mining companies.
The researchers mapped the Agta ancestral domain as overlapping both the mining concession and the Northern
Sierra Madre Natural Park. That does not mean the active mine itself is inside the park.

## What residents say

The 2006 agreement promised the Agta cash assistance, royalties, scholarships and priority employment.
Researchers working in Dinapigue from 2013 to 2015 found that residents disputed whether those promises had
been met and whether later operators had obtained consent. Some Agta wanted mining stopped in damaged or
culturally sensitive areas. Others said mine work had become necessary to pay daily and school costs.

GMA reported similarly divided views in 2025. Some residents credited the mine with jobs, fencing and school
projects. A farmer attributed lower harvests to mine waste reaching irrigation water. That is the resident's
account; the report did not establish the cause through water testing.

## What the government and company say

Republic Act No. 9125 created the Northern Sierra Madre Natural Park and its buffer zones to protect habitats,
water and the rights of resident Indigenous communities. It bars mineral exploration and extraction within the
park and buffer zones. Dinapigue Mining Corporation told GMA that its operation is outside the protected area,
with a 1.1-kilometre buffer. GMA said the government agencies that permitted the mine declined its interview
requests for that report.

Nickel Asia calls Dinapigue one of its operating mines. It reported 0.3 million wet metric tonnes of Dinapigue
ore exports in 2025. The filing says the group's high-iron limonite and much of its mid-grade saprolite go to
Chinese customers, but it does not identify the destination of Dinapigue's shipments separately.
