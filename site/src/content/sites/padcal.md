## What the images show

There is no "before mining" image here. Padcal had been operating for 30 years when the first usable
Landsat scenes were taken. In the 1988–1990 composite, a pale scar on the left of the box sits over the ore
body, and a grey tailings pond lies near the middle. By 2026 that middle pond has grown over with
vegetation, which fits Philex's report that TSF 1 and 2 were decommissioned and rehabilitated. A much
larger grey-white tailings surface now fills the valleys to the southeast. It is the biggest bare area in
the box. The magenta layer marks the land that went from green vegetation to bare ground or water.

The Sentinel-2 pair starts after the 2012 spill and shows the more recent change, mostly at the edges of
the southeastern tailings area.

## A mine and a mine camp

Construction began in 1957 and production in 1958. The mine first used both an open pit and underground
workings, then shifted to full underground mining in 1963. The ore body is in Sitio Padcal, Barangay Camp 3,
Tuba. The mill and the three tailings facilities are in Barangay Ampucao, Itogon. Camp 3 includes the Philex
mine camp, where mining is the main source of income; agriculture remains important in its outlying sitios.

## Who lives here

Philex's 2016 sustainability report identifies Camp 3 and Ampucao as the two host communities. It describes
Ibaloi people as the original settlers of Tuba and says Kankanaey people later migrated there. The company
also describes today's mine camp as a community of families from different parts of the Philippines.

## The tailings facilities and the river

Padcal has used three tailings storage facilities. Philex reported in 2016 that TSF 1 and TSF 2 had been
decommissioned and were being rehabilitated. TSF 3 failed in August 2012. A DENR river-basin report records
about 20 million tonnes of tailings entering Balog Creek, which flows into the Agno River. The MGB stopped
mine operations, and the Pollution Adjudication Board imposed separate water-pollution fines.

## What the government and company say

The MGB first allowed limited operations for urgent repairs to TSF 3. In August 2014 it permanently lifted the
cease-and-desist order, saying the issues from the spill had been substantially addressed. Philex says Padcal
remains operating and projects mining through December 2028. Its principal MPSA is valid until January 2034;
the permit term and the company's projected mine life are different dates.
